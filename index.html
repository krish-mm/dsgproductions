In the room

Setups that change how the night feels.

LED dance floor

The floor itself becomes part of the show.

A dance floor built from LED panels that light up in colors and patterns. It pulls people onto it, and it photographs from every angle.

If you love the venue’s floor as it is, or you want a quieter, more classic look, you can skip it. A custom vinyl wrap is another option when you want names, a monogram, or a design underfoot.

  • LED panels
  • Color & pattern looks
  • Custom vinyl wraps

LED video wall

A backdrop that changes with every part of the night.

A large screen built from LED panels, used for visuals, names, monograms, photos, or live video. It gives the stage a presence that can shift from dinner to dancing to a formal program.

In a small, intimate room, a large screen can take over the space — we’ll tell you if it isn’t the right fit.

  • Visuals & monograms
  • Photos & live video
  • Stage backdrop

Lighting & effects

Color on the walls. Sparks on the moment.

Uplights wash walls, drapes, and columns in your colors. Moving heads pan, tilt, and throw beams and patterns programmed to hit with the music. Cold sparks frame the big moments: grand entrances, first dances, and the last song.

We also run spark guns, CO2, low-lying fog (the “dance on cloud” look), lasers, a snow machine, a bubble machine, confetti cannons, CO2 jets, PAR lights, spotlights, custom monograms and name washes, and a custom DJ booth.

  • Moving heads
  • Cold sparks

How it works

From the first note to the last song.
