Production

The full setup, built for your room.

Every event is custom. Tell us the events, the venue, and the crowd, and we build the quote around them after a free consultation.

LED dance floor

A dance floor built from LED panels that light up in colors and patterns. The floor itself becomes part of the show and pulls people onto it.

When to skip itIf you love your venue’s floor as it is, or you want a classic, understated look.

Vinyl wraps

Custom printed vinyl laid over the dance floor with your names, monogram, or design. It makes the floor match the event and shows up well in photos from above.

When to skip itIf you are already on an LED dance floor, or the floor won’t be seen much.

LED video wall

A large screen made of LED panels for visuals, names, monograms, photos, or live video. It gives the stage a backdrop that can change with every part of the night.

When to skip itFor a small, intimate room where a big screen would take over the space.

Sound systems

Audio for the room — dinner, speaking moments, and the dance floor. We bring our own power stations. Specific speaker models and system sizes are set in the consultation, based on the venue and the crowd.

Also availableA custom DJ booth, and a team of 5+ DJs so there is always backup.

Moving-head lights & uplighting

Uplights set on the floor wash walls, drapes, and columns in your colors. Moving heads pan, tilt, and change color, throwing beams and patterns programmed to hit with the music. Uplights are the simplest way to change how the whole room looks, from dinner through the last song. Moving heads make the dance floor feel like a concert.

We also use PAR lights, spotlights, and custom monograms and name washes.

When to skip itIf your venue already has built-in lighting you love, or it is an outdoor daytime event where the color won’t show. Skip the moving heads for a quiet ceremony or a sit-down dinner where you want the room calm and still.

Effects

We set the effects up before you walk in, and we trigger them when the moment hits.

Cold sparks. Machines that shoot fountains of sparks for a fireworks look without real fireworks. They frame grand entrances, first dances, and the last song. Check with the venue on ceiling height and whether they’re allowed. Outdoors, they depend on wind and weather.

Spark guns. A handheld spark effect, aimed and fired by hand right on the drop. Puts a burst of sparkle where the energy is. Check with the venue first; skip outdoors or at a low-key event.

CO2 guns. Handheld blasters that shoot a cold white plume of CO2 over the crowd. Fired on the drop, they give the dance floor a jolt. Skip them if you want a quieter, more formal vibe.

Dance on cloud. Thick fog that stays low and hugs the floor — the dancing-on-the-clouds look for a first dance. Less of a fit for a high-energy party where nobody is doing a slow dance.

Also in the lineup: lasers, a snow machine, a bubble machine, confetti cannons, and CO2 jets.

Questions

Asked and answered.

How much does it cost?

Every event is custom, so pricing comes after a free consultation. Your quote depends on how many events, how many hours, and what production you add. Send the form to book a consult.

Do you travel?

Yes. We’re based in Birmingham, AL and serve events all across the USA. The travel fee depends on the distance and the gear, and your free consultation sets it.

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend booking at least 6 months before your event. A 35% deposit locks your date.

Can we send must-play and do-not-play lists?

Yes. Your must-plays are locked in and your do-not-plays stay off. We read the room for everything in between.

Are you insured?

Yes. We’re insured for $1M.

What if the power or the DJ falls through?

Power: we bring our own power stations, and our lights run on batteries. DJ: we’ve got a team of 5+ DJs, so there’s always backup.

Any effects you’d recommend?

Cold sparks frame the big moments: grand entrances, first dances, and the last song. The rest of the lineup is under Production, and we can help you choose what fits the room.

Is there a guest minimum?

We don’t usually do events under 50 guests, so we may not be the best fit for a smaller gathering. You can still send the form and we’ll let you know.
